# Blood Sugar

Blood sugar, or blood glucose, is the primary source of energy for the body. It is measured in milligrams per deciliter (mg/dL) or millimoles per liter (mmol/L).

**Fasting Blood Sugar:**
- Normal: 70-99 mg/dL
- Prediabetes: 100-125 mg/dL
- Diabetes: 126 mg/dL or higher

**Postprandial (2 hours after eating):**
- Normal: Less than 140 mg/dL
- Prediabetes: 140-199 mg/dL
- Diabetes: 200 mg/dL or higher

Low blood sugar (hypoglycemia) is below 70 mg/dL and can cause shakiness, confusion, and fainting. High blood sugar (hyperglycemia) over time damages blood vessels and nerves.
