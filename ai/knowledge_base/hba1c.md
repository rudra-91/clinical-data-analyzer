# HbA1c

HbA1c, or glycated hemoglobin, measures average blood glucose over the past 2 to 3 months. It is reported as a percentage.

**Normal:** Below 5.7%

**Prediabetes:** 5.7% to 6.4%

**Diabetes:** 6.5% or higher

For people with diabetes, the target HbA1c is often below 7%, though individualized goals may vary. HbA1c does not require fasting and is less affected by short-term stress or illness compared to daily glucose tests.

Elevated HbA1c increases risk of diabetic complications including retinopathy, nephropathy, and cardiovascular disease.
